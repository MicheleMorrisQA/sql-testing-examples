# Professional Experience

## Career Overview

My career spans more than 20 years in Quality Assurance and Quality Engineering, with experience across FinTech, banking technology, SaaS, HealthTech, utilities, and large enterprise platforms.

Over the course of my career, my role has grown from hands-on software testing into technical investigation, quality leadership, automation, release management, risk assessment, and broader quality strategy.

A significant part of my work has involved determining what is actually happening when something does not work as expected. I have investigated financial transactions, application behavior, data issues, APIs, integrations, production problems, defects, and complex business workflows.

I use tools and technical evidence such as SQL, Postman, application workflows, test data, logs, dashboards, and transaction information to understand what happened and determine where a problem originates.

I look beyond the application itself because an issue may be related to data, configuration, an API, a third-party vendor, an integration, or an operational process rather than application code.

I enjoy this type of investigation because it requires understanding the entire process rather than focusing only on an individual system.

# Technical Investigation & Problem Solving

Technical investigation has become one of the strongest parts of my experience.

Throughout my career, I have frequently been the person who investigates a problem when the answer is not immediately obvious.

My approach includes:

1. Understanding the expected behavior
2. Reproducing the issue when possible
3. Comparing expected and actual behavior
4. Reviewing application behavior and available evidence
5. Examining data and transaction information
6. Investigating APIs and integrations
7. Reviewing logs and other technical information when available
8. Determining the scope and potential business impact
9. Working with the appropriate technical teams
10. Validating the implemented solution

Tools and information I have used during these investigations include:

* SQL
* Postman
* Browser developer tools
* Test data
* Application workflows
* Logs
* Dashboards
* Transaction information
* Database information
* API requests and responses

I also spend significant time translating technical findings for different audiences.

Engineering teams may need specific technical evidence, while Product, Compliance, or Operations teams may need to understand the business impact and what needs to happen next.

I believe that resolving an issue is more important than simply assigning it to another team. My approach is to stay involved until there is a clear understanding of the problem, the appropriate resolution has been implemented, and the result has been validated.
